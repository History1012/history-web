    Key Terms

  • Capitalism - an economic system in which private individuals or groups own the resources and produce goods for a profit.

  • Red Scare - a time when the federal government targeted suspected communists, anarchists, and radicals.

  • Radicals - people who hold extreme political views.

  • American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU) - organization dedicated to defending the individual rights and freedoms of all Americans.

  • Anti-Defamation League (ADL) - organization dedicated to exsposing and countering the violence of extremist groups, such as the Ku Klux Klan.

    • Important People

  • A. Mitchell Palmer Attorney General who obtained funding from Congress to establish a division in the Department of Justice to hunt for radicals, naming J. Edgar Hoover as its head.

  • J. Edgar Hoover J. Edgar Hoover was the first FBI Director.
